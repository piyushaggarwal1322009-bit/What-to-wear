# Product Documentation: What Should I Wear?

**Project Purpose:**
A practical, context-aware weather recommendation website that tells users what to wear based on the weather and what they are doing.

**Concept:**
User enters Location + Situation. The application obtains weather information and produces recommendations on what to wear, carry, and avoid, and explains why.

**Situations:**
- College
- Interview
- Wedding
- Gym
- Outdoor Event

**Core Requirement:**
A complete functional MVP. Connect a real weather API to a rule-based recommendation engine.

**Priorities:**
Don't recommend what looks best. Recommend what makes the most sense for the user's situation and the conditions.
