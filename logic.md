# Logic & Rules

## Temperature
- <15°C: Cold
- 15-21°C: Cool
- 22-27°C: Comfortable
- 28-31°C: Warm
- >31°C: Hot

## Priorities per Situation
- **College**: High comfort, high practicality.
- **Interview**: Very high formality, high weather protection.
- **Wedding**: Very high formality, medium comfort.
- **Gym**: Very high breathability, very high comfort.
- **Outdoor Event**: Very high weather protection, high comfort.
